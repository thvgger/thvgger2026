# Thvgger visual direction

A clean, professional portfolio with a curious side. The spacious typography and navigation of https://www.paulkalkbrenner.net/ inform the restraint; Thvgger's own striped cube, original work, and interactive details provide the personality.

Tokens: white #ffffff, black #000000, muted ink #565656, light canvas #f3f3f3, and rule #dedede. Preserve the established variable Inter family and large wordmark. Supporting headings use a fluid 36–64px scale, body text 16–20px, and small navigation 12–13px. Use generous space with tighter groups around related information.

Homepage layout: a spacious name-and-cube opening with a direct role statement and a scroll link. Two selected projects sit in an offset image composition: Studio Space on the left, the Thvgger identity on the right. A soft-gray section connects a personal introduction with expandable identity, interface, and development notes. A large contact invitation ends the page. The reading flow is opening → work → person and practice → contact, on an ordinary scrolling document.

Studio Space presentation: original Figma vectors lead a dedicated case study, informed by the user's Quaandry logo-variation guide. The portfolio shell stays white and black in Inter. Artwork boards use the source identity's ink #080808, pink #F20D5E, and light #F6F4EE. Lead with a large wordmark and its family; follow with short explanations and spacious examples of primary, stacked, symbol, favicon, colour, digital placements, and clear space. Keep explanatory text in responsive HTML and preserve the complete source SVGs. Mark the project as in progress. Do not infer a client, industry, commission, or outcome from the file name.

All main pages remain separate: Work, About, Contact, Playground, and individual projects. Homepage content introduces these destinations rather than replacing them. Playground stays a Coming soon placeholder.

Design review: avoid turning the new sections into equal service cards, repeated oversized headings, or decorative counters. The custom cube and offset project images carry the identity. Keep the surrounding navigation and copy disciplined. Let the work provide the imagery rather than adding unrelated stock or fabricated client projects.

Motion: the homepage restores the original large-cube opening, shrink, and name-push sequence in a refined 1.86-second entrance. A controlled cube turn, masked name reveal, and transform-based composition settle into the existing wordmark without changing layout dimensions. Supporting copy and the scroll link enter last. Navigation stays available, and scroll, touch, keyboard, pointer input, resizing, or a hidden tab settles the intro immediately. Reduced motion skips the choreography. All content is server-rendered; the CSS sequence also finishes when JavaScript is unavailable. Retain direct cube manipulation, gentle image responses, plus-to-close rotation on native expandable notes, and the circular contact link. Native scrollbar chrome is hidden while ordinary page scrolling remains available. No repeated scroll reveals or scroll hijacking.

Content: describe original personal work honestly. No music-template references, outside client brands, invented results, location, years of experience, or assumed credentials. GitHub points to the user's account; email remains the explicitly requested placeholder.
