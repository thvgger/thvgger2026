# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors exploring Thvgger's personal design and development work, including potential collaborators and clients.

## Product Purpose

Present original work clearly and give visitors a direct route to contact Thvgger or explore his GitHub. The user wants a clean, spacious, multi-page portfolio inspired by paulkalkbrenner.net's typography and navigation, with his own work speaking for itself. The homepage must also scroll through useful content: selected work, a personal introduction, capabilities, and a contact invitation.

## Brand Personality

Precise, sculptural, curious. Continue the monochrome palette and striped cube identity. Use generous white space and considered type rather than dense panels or additional effects.

## Structure

Home, Work, About, Contact, and Playground are separate pages. Each project has its own detail page. FOOLY COOLY has a dedicated storefront case study and leads the Work page. It is featured alongside Studio Space on the homepage. Studio Space has a dedicated visual identity guide using the user's Figma artwork, labelled as in progress. FOOLY COOLY is a personal prototype with sample product photography, browsing, and a demo cart; checkout is outside its scope. Playground remains a Coming soon placeholder until the user supplies its direction.

## Anti-references

Music-template content, named-brand work presented as client experience, replacing all dedicated pages with one page, busy interface panels, and active playground features.

## Design Principles

- Show actual work and label personal studies honestly.
- Make the homepage worth scrolling, with calm spacing and clear navigation.
- Let project images lead the Work page.
- Keep interaction subtle and useful.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use keyboard navigation, visible focus, native mobile-menu dialog behavior, readable text, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion preferences. These are implementation defaults rather than a claim of certified accessibility conformance.

Hide native page scrollbar chrome at the user's request while preserving ordinary scrolling and the visible Scroll to explore link. The homepage intro must settle immediately on input and skip motion for visitors who request it.
